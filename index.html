Our principles

Meta is building technology that connects you to people, interests and experiences that matter to you. Our principles embody what we stand for and guide our approach to how we build.

a

Give people a voice

We believe that open expression and free discourse enable progress. We work to empower billions of voices around the world to share what matters most to them safely and authentically.

Global Impact Free Expression
b

Build connection and community

Our services help people form meaningful relationships, discover communities of shared interest, and bridge physical distance through immersive real-time technologies.

Community First Meaningful Links
c

Serve everyone

We design products accessible to everyone across diverse regions, devices, and internet conditions. Inclusion is at the heart of every technology feature we build.

Inclusive Design Universal Access
d

Keep people safe and protect privacy

We invest heavily in privacy-first architectures, robust encryption, and advanced AI safety controls to guard individual privacy and protect communities online.

Privacy First Advanced Security
e

Promote economic opportunity

We provide free tools and platforms that enable millions of entrepreneurs, small businesses, and creators to reach customers, scale their ventures, and thrive globally.

Creator Economy Business Growth
